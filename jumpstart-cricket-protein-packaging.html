  • Jumpstart

    Logo / Packaging

    Jumpstart is a quality, performance and sustainability-focused company that ethically produces and sells cricket-based protein powder. They help athletes who want a sustainable, delicious and high-quality protein supplement to achieve their fitness goals.

  • The final logo combines mountain peaks, which symbolize goal-setting and sustainability, with cricket legs, that point to both the source of the protein and the name of the brand. This mark is to represent how this product jumpstarts your progress towards your own goals.
