  • Reveries

    Print / Social Media

    Reveries is a live-music, dance and poetry fundraising event hosted by Soma Anima Arts—a Vancouver-based interdisciplinary arts organization dedicated to fostering compassion, liberation, and community through dance and live performance art.

    The goal was to create a visual identity to be used for print material and social media. The colours, type and photo treatment were chosen to illustrate the meaning of the word “reverie,” which is synonymous with the word “daydream.”
